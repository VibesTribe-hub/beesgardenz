Our Services & Pricing (Toronto & GTA)

☀️

1. Summer Maintenance

Includes our essential weekly care package: professional mowing, detailed weed trimming around obstacles, and clean edging along sidewalks and flower beds for a pristine finish.

Pricing Breakdown:

  • Lawn Mowing (Per Visit, One-Time) $50 - $100
  • Seasonal Package (Approx. 20-25 visits) $1,200 - $1,800
  • Note: Prices depend heavily on the size of the lawn and property accessibility.
🍂

2. Fall Cleanup Services

Comprehensive fall care designed to prepare your yard for the harsh winter months, including final cuts, debris removal, and winterizing plant beds.

Pricing Breakdown:

  • Standard Fall Cleanup (General Debris) $200 - $500 per service
  • Dedicated Leaf Removal $150 - $400 per service
  • Hourly Rate (2-Person Crew) Approx. $130 per hour
  • Note: Price is based on the volume of leaves and property layout.
❄️

3. Winter Safety & Access

Prompt and reliable snow and ice management services ensuring your driveways, walkways, and access points remain safe and clear all season long.

Pricing Breakdown:

  • Seasonal Snow Removal Contract $575 - $1,725 (Nov-Mar)
  • Per-Visit Snow Removal (On-Demand) $45 - $90 per visit
  • De-icing Application (Add-on) $60 - $175 per application

Important Pricing Disclaimer

The prices listed on this page are for informational purposes and represent estimated average costs in the Toronto and GTA area. Your **final quote may vary** based on your property's specific requirements. Please contact us to schedule a **free, on-site consultation**.
